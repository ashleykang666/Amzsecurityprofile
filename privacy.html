Private-use notice: The application is designed solely for advertising accounts owned by or expressly authorized to its operator. No public sign-up, third-party seller onboarding, or consumer checkout is offered on this website.

1. About the application

Amazon Ads AI Manager (the “Application”) is an independently developed tool for the operator's own Amazon advertising activities. Its intended functions include campaign performance analysis, keyword insights, advertising cost monitoring, and owner-controlled optimization of bids, targeting, and budgets where supported and authorized.

This Privacy Policy describes the information that may be processed as such functions are configured. The Application is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Amazon.

2. Information we may access

Depending on approved API access and account permissions, the Application may process:

  • Amazon advertising account and profile identifiers.
  • Campaigns, ad groups, keywords, targeting rules, bids, and budgets.
  • Advertising metrics such as impressions, clicks, cost, attributed orders or sales, and derived performance indicators.
  • Product identifiers relevant to the operator's advertising analysis.
  • Authorization tokens and diagnostic information needed to operate the approved integration.

The Application is not intended to collect shopper names, mailing addresses, card details, or unrelated customer personal information.

3. How information is used

Information is used only for the operator's internal advertising analysis and management, including monitoring performance, producing reports, evaluating advertising efficiency and estimated profitability, and identifying potential campaign improvements.

If automated campaign adjustments are enabled, they are intended to follow the operator's defined settings and applicable Amazon API permissions.

4. Amazon authorization

Access to Amazon advertising data requires the applicable Amazon authorization process, such as Login with Amazon, and any API access approval required by Amazon. The Application is intended to connect only advertising accounts that the operator owns or is authorized to manage.

Users should not enter Amazon login passwords into this informational website. Authorization may be revoked through the relevant Amazon account controls, subject to Amazon's applicable authorization behavior.

5. Sharing and service providers

The Application is not designed to sell or publicly disclose advertising account data. Depending on how the private system is deployed, hosting, storage, and AI-processing services may be used to perform authorized functions. Any service-provider access must be assessed and limited according to applicable Amazon API terms and data protection obligations.

6. Data security

The operator is responsible for securing the deployed system, restricting access to advertising data, and protecting authorization credentials and tokens with safeguards appropriate to the actual environment. API credentials, access tokens, and confidential advertising data must not be placed in a public GitHub repository or in this website's source code.

7. Data retention and deletion

Authorized data may be retained for the time needed to operate the Application, analyze historical advertising performance, or address legitimate maintenance needs. The operator controls its own deployed storage and should delete data or disconnect the integration when no longer required, in accordance with applicable platform rules and legal obligations.

8. This public information website

This static website presents application information and this Privacy Policy. It does not offer account registration, collect form submissions, or include application-managed analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub Pages, if used for hosting, may process technical connection information under its own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features, integrations, and actual data practices evolve. Significant changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
